Sir Fisher, on the Fortaleza seafront

We face the sea on Avenida Beira Mar, where many people in Fortaleza walk in the evening. The menu includes fish, prawns, Brazilian dishes and a few recipes inspired by the British side of our name.

We are across from the Japanese Garden, within walking distance of most Beira-Mar hotels. Late in the afternoon, the tables face the sunset over the seafront.

What comes to the table

Prawns, fish, cuts of meat to share and small plates for the table. Our fish and chips is made with pescada amarela, or acoupa weakfish, caught off the coast of Ceará. Read more on the English fish and chips page.

Prices are in Brazilian reais. As a guide, small plates and sandwiches cost R$ 27 to R$ 60, platters to share R$ 62 to R$ 99, cocktails R$ 18 to R$ 30 and beers R$ 11 to R$ 24. The weekday set lunch runs from R$ 31 to R$ 52. A 10% service charge is added to the bill and is optional.

Late afternoon here

The tables face the Meireles seafront, with a clear view of the late-afternoon light.

In Fortaleza the sun goes down between 5:25 pm and 5:56 pm depending on the month. The city sits close to the equator, so the time changes little across the year. The light starts to turn about 40 minutes earlier. For a table facing the water, come around 4:00 pm.

Do I need to book?

You can book or walk in. Check available times for up to ten guests on the booking site. Same-day online bookings close at 3:30 pm, Fortaleza time; after that, service is first come, first served.

For groups of 11 to 29, message the team on WhatsApp. For events with 30 to 100 seated guests, request a proposal (in Portuguese).

Small and medium-sized pets are welcome at the outdoor tables.

Is English spoken?

Service is mainly in Portuguese. The menu is available in English, with the Portuguese name of each dish to point to, and the booking site works in English. You can also message us on WhatsApp before you come.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Elo, American Express, UnionPay and Cabal, including foreign cards and contactless payments. Cash, Pix and major meal vouchers are accepted too. Check your meal voucher brand with the team.

Where we are

Facing the sea on the Meireles seafront, across from the Japanese Garden. Most Beira-Mar hotels are a walk away along the promenade.
